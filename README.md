💰 FinTrack — Personal Finance & Investment Tracker

Manage your budget, track your expenses, and monitor your investment portfolio in one place.

📖 Overview

FinTrack is a personal finance application designed to help users manage their money without relying on spreadsheets. It combines monthly budgeting, income and expense tracking, and investment portfolio monitoring in a single interface.

The application supports tracking assets such as stock market indexes, gold, and cryptocurrencies, helping users get a clearer overview of their financial situation.

✨ Features

📊 Investment Portfolio Tracking: Add assets, record quantities and purchase prices, and monitor portfolio performance.

🧾 Monthly Budget Management: Record income and expenses and organize transactions by category.

📈 Financial Dashboard: Get an overview of spending, investments, and net worth.

🖥️ Installable Desktop Application: Download and install the application using the latest available release.

🚀 Installation

Open the Releases section of this repository.

Download the installer for the latest version.

Run the installer and follow the setup instructions.

Launch FinTrack and start managing your finances.

🧭 Getting Started

Create your first monthly budget.

Add assets to your investment portfolio, including their quantities and purchase prices.

Record your income and expenses by category.

Use the main dashboard to review your financial overview.
